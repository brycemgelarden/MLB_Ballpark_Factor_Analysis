# MLB_Ballpark_Factor_Analysis

Exploration of Major League Baseball ballpark environments and the variances in both pitch type frequencies and performance.

Randomly sampled ~175,000 swings over the 2023, 2024, and 2025 seasons at 29 ballparks.

**Research Question**: Do MLB ballpark environments explain variance in pitch type frequencies and pitch type contact quality across the 2023, 2024, and 2025 seasons (post pitch clock implementation and defensive shift ban era)?
